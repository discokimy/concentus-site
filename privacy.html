Concentus Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

In short: Concentus does not collect, store or share any personal information. Everything you do in the app stays on your device, and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own private iCloud account, which we cannot see.

Information we collect

None. Concentus has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not send any information about you or how you use it to the developer or to anyone else.

Microphone

The tuner, the intonation check and the voice tools use your microphone to hear the pitch of your instrument or voice. The sound is analyzed instantly, on your device, and then discarded. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere, unless you choose to record yourself (see Recordings below). The microphone is only active while the Tuner, a voice exercise or the recorder is open, and you can turn off access at any time in your device's Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

Recordings

Recording is optional: Concentus records only when you tap Record (in the recorder, Record and Compare, or a shortcut you set up). Each recording, its layers, its name and note, and the pitch measurements Record and Compare makes from it are saved only in the app's own storage on your device, as audio files. Recordings are never uploaded or synced, not even through iCloud, and they are left out of your device's backups. A recording leaves your device only when you choose to share or export it, for example with the share sheet or by dragging it out on a Mac, and then only to the place you pick. You can delete recordings at any time, one at a time or several at once; they are then removed from your device and can't be recovered. Deleting the app deletes all recordings.

Information stored on your device

Your settings, saved chord progressions, custom tunings and instruments, string-change logs, practice log (minutes practiced and your streak), ear-training and note-reading scores and vocal range are stored on your device. They are included in your device's own backups (for example iCloud Backup) if you have those turned on; that is handled by Apple under your Apple account's settings, not by Concentus. Deleting the app deletes this information from that device; if you use iCloud sync (below), your other devices and your iCloud account keep their copy.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, Concentus can keep the information above the same on your iPhone, iPad and Mac. It does this with Apple's iCloud key-value storage, in your own private iCloud account: the data goes from your devices to your iCloud account and back, under your Apple account's protection. It never goes to a server of ours, and we have no way to see it. Recordings are never part of it: they stay on the device where you made them. You can turn sync off at any time in the app under Settings → iCloud; your information then stays on each device. Turning off iCloud for Concentus in your device's Settings, or signing out of iCloud, also stops it.

Widgets, controls and Siri

The Concentus widget shows your practice streak, minutes this week and how old your strings are. The app shares that small summary with its widget on your device only. Shortcuts and Siri requests such as "How old are my strings?" are answered on your device from the app's own information.

Notifications

If you choose to be reminded about string changes, or to get a daily practice reminder, the reminders are scheduled on your device. No information is sent to a server to do this.

Third parties

Concentus contains no third-party code that collects data: no analytics services, advertising networks or social media tools.

Children

Concentus is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Any change that involves collecting information would be explained in the app before it happens.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@concentus.app.
